# infused books

tired of spending hours hunting for specific enchanted books? setting up a trading hall just to get mending? infused books adds crafting recipes for every enchantment in the game, so you can get the books you need without the grind.

every recipe follows a simple formula: **book + bottle o' enchanting + thematic materials.** no random enchantment table rolls, no waiting for the right librarian, no trading hall required.

to get higher levels, combine two books of the same level in an anvil, just like vanilla. this keeps progression fair while still giving you control over what you get.

## design philosophy

recipes are meant to feel thematic and fair, not trivial. materials relate to what the enchantment does -- knockback uses a piston, mending uses a ghast tear (ender crystals heal the dragon, and they're made with ghast tears), soul speed uses soul sand. higher levels cost more through the anvil system rather than progressively rarer ingredients, keeping things consistent.

---

## compatibility

works with any mod that doesn't mess with the vanilla crafting system. fully compatible with rei, emi, and jei for recipe viewing.
